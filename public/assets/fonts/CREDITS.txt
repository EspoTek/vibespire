Vibespire bitmap fonts (embedded as data in src/render/font8x8.ts and src/render/fontSmall.ts)

8x8 UI font
  font8x8_basic, https://github.com/dhepper/font8x8, by Daniel Hepper
  Based on Marcel Sondaar's font8_8 and the IBM public-domain VGA fonts.

  Extra glyphs (arrows, heart, pips, circles, block, middle dot) drawn for Vibespire.
