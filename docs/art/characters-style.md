# Vibespire v2 character style bible

Target: front-on Phantasy Star (Sega Master System, 1987), using the measured enemy rules in `docs/research/art-style.md`. Preserve each existing creature and its class/act identity. This is a character pass, not a new aesthetic.

- **Light:** upper left, approximately 10 o'clock. Highlight upper/left planes, use a broad middle tone, put the deepest material shade on lower/right planes. Eyes and furnace cores may be self-lit; no painted glow or cast ground shadow.
- **Outline:** one logical pixel of pure `#000000` on exposed silhouette edges. Black internal breaks distinguish limbs, jaws, armour plates and facial features. Two-pixel black pockets are fine at joints, not a uniform two-pixel halo. No coloured exterior fringe, antialiasing or partial alpha.
- **Ramps:** two or three hard steps per material, excluding the black outline. Blue `#0000AA/#0055FF/#00AAFF`; turquoise `#005555/#00AAAA/#55FFFF`; green `#005500/#00AA00/#AAFF00`; steel `#555555/#AAAAAA/#FFFFFF`; violet `#5500AA/#AA00FF/#AA55FF`; heat `#AA0000/#FF5500/#FFFF55`. Select a few ramps, not all of them. Every RGB channel must be 00/55/AA/FF. Never use the magenta source key on a subject.
- **Budget:** at most 15 opaque colours plus transparency for an enemy or 64px portrait; aim at 10–12 for repairs. At most 12 opaque colours for a HUD portrait. Spend a colour on the face before decorative highlights.
- **Canvas and scale:** retain every existing filename and canvas. All 37 non-boss sprites are 64×64; the nine bosses are 96×96. Centre the visible body, keep the last opaque row at `height-1`. A minion's main mass should occupy about 38–48px of the 64px canvas (60–75%); its antennae or tendrils can reach 56px. Normal bodies 48–58px (75–91%); elites 56–62px (88–97%); bosses 86–94px of 96px (90–98%). These describe the longest visible dimension; narrow/tall creatures need not fill a square. Leave a one-pixel side/top safety margin where anatomy permits. Do not enlarge a minion just to fill empty space.
- **Silhouette and richness:** preserve the original pose, anatomy, layered construction and material detail. Dense machinery, engraved plates, ribs, filaments, tendril fans and crown ornaments are part of the Phantasy Star identity. Remove unsupported shade speckles, not these features. Keep limb gaps at least two pixels when anatomy permits; claws, fingers and chains must survive at 1×. Deliberate floating pieces remain when part of the concept. Never simplify a rich original into a generic cartoon to satisfy a palette or cluster metric.
- **Eyes/faces:** front-on. At 64px, readable 2–4px eyes, black sockets and a clear mouth are more useful than many teeth. At 32px use purpose-drawn eyes with a single black pupil, a connected brow, a one-pixel nose shadow and a 2–3px mouth. Reserve a minimum 12px-wide face. Preserve Breaker's swept blue hair/blue-violet armour, Graft's teal hair/living green growth/violet flower, and Chronist's blue hood/cyan monocle/gold clasp.
- **Dithering:** none on characters. Use solid connected clusters. No checker shading, noise or scattered highlight pixels. Stars, spores and energy fragments must be few, intentional and outlined; they are not surface texture.
- **Review:** always inspect actual-size art on both a neutral field and its act backdrop, plus exact 4× NEAREST. Combat normally draws 64px enemies at 2×, but the entire line drops to 1× for 4+ enemies or a boss plus three minions. Review small enemies in a dedicated 1× backdrop row and in crowded lines. Palette compliance alone does not earn a fidelity score of four. Score detail density against the original independently of cleanliness; every replacement must retain at least the original's meaningful detail.
- **Cleanup first:** work on the original native pixels. Keep silhouettes, structural black ink and characteristic fittings; consolidate redundant material shades and relocate unsupported highlights. Regenerate only if cleanup cannot reach four, using the original as a visual reference and requiring its detail density, menace and composition.

The 64px class-select portraits remain unchanged identity references. Crop the head and upper chest, downsample deliberately and hand-clean eyes, brows, hair silhouette and class signifiers at 32px. The face should fill most of the frame. Use only colours from that character's master, with clean skin and no invented stubble. The reproducible crop/feature edits live in `chars_portraits.py`; the old `portraits.py` whole-bust downscale is not the HUD pipeline.
